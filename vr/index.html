Controls and about these free VR samples

Three arcade game samples in your browser

Explore Cubes, Cube Snake and Pongo in a virtual arcade. Open this page in a compatible headset browser and select Enter VR, or explore the 3D preview on a computer. These are simplified browser samples; the videos show the full Quest game.

Cubes thumbstick controls

Use the left thumbstick to move a piece left, right, forward or back. Flick the right thumbstick left or right for a 90-degree turn. Press A/X to start, then hard-drop a piece; B/Y restarts the sample.

3D Snake thumbstick controls

Use the left thumbstick to steer left, right, forward or back. Use the right thumbstick up or down to climb or descend. A/X starts; B/Y restarts. The snake wraps at the edges of its 3D board and grows when it eats food.

Pongo and hand controls

Point at the Pongo board to position your horizontal paddle and bounce the ball upward at floating targets. For any sample, point at its on-screen controls and pinch a tracked hand or press a controller trigger. Use the floor circles to teleport around the room.
