Quick Match

Pick Cubes, Cube Snake, Pongo, or Any Game. Join a compatible match or browse Open Rooms and host your own.

Player invites

Pick someone from Available Players and send an invite. Mark yourself available if you’d like other players to invite you.

Private codes

Playing with a friend? Pick the same game, then have one player host and share the six-character code. The other enters it to join.

Learn by playing.

Open Help / Pause in any game to practice hands or Touch controllers. Animated 3D hands show the motion, and the game plays a click when it recognizes your input. Skip a step or replay whenever you like.

Cubes

Practice moving a piece one cell at a time, turning it 90 degrees, and landing a hard drop with a flat-hand chop.

Cube Snake

Practice steering with your left hand, then curl your right fingers palm up to climb or palm down to descend.

Pongo

Move a practice paddle with your left hand, then tilt your right wrist to aim the rebound.

BEFORE YOU PLAY

Good to know.

What’s included?

Cubes, Cube Snake, and Pongo are part of the TableTop Cubes collection. Cubes includes Classic, Relaxed, and three-minute Sprint modes.

Where can I play?

Find TableTop Cubes on the Meta Horizon Store for Meta Quest. The store listing has current supported headsets, pricing, and release details.

Is this a multiplayer game?

Yes. Use Online Play to find an opponent, send an invite, or join a friend with a private code. Both players need an updated build for the new matchmaking options.

Can I move or resize the board with my hands?

Pinch and lift with one hand to move the board. Pinch with both hands and bring them together or apart to resize it in supported screens.

How do I get help or share an idea?

For a controls refresher, open Help / Pause and select Practice Controls, or Practice / Watch Demo in Pongo. To share feedback, email MikeBlue247Dev@gmail.com. We’d love to hear what you’d like to play next.
