BEFORE YOU PLAY

Good to know.

What’s included?

Cubes, Cube Snake, and Pongo are part of the TableTop Cubes collection. Cubes includes Classic, Relaxed, and three-minute Sprint modes.

Where can I play?

Find TableTop Cubes on the Meta Horizon Store for Meta Quest. The store listing has current supported headsets, pricing, and release details.

Is this a multiplayer game?

The collection is designed for solo play. Online rankings let you compare scores without a live multiplayer match.

How do I get help or share an idea?

Use the in-game instructions and help screens, or email MikeBlue247Dev@gmail.com. We’d love to hear what you’d like to play next.
