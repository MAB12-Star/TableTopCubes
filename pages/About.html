TableTop Cubes is a mixed-reality arcade collection from CC Creations. It brings three solo games into the space around you: Cubes, Cube Snake, and Pongo.

Three games. One table.

Stack and clear in Cubes, eat and grow in Cube Snake, or bounce and aim in Pongo. The redesigned collection brings matching boards, clear menus, and a shared home hub together with personal bests and rankings.

Cubes gives you three ways to play: Classic, Relaxed, and a three-minute Sprint. Next-piece previews, hold, combos, and floor-clear bonuses add choices to every run.
